Each sequence inside Metulifer hosts its own dedicated audio synthesis engine encapsulated within the `Voice` class (`Source/DSP/Voice.{h,cpp}`).
All audio code strictly adheres to hard real-time audio constraints: **zero dynamic memory allocations**, **zero mutex locks**, and **zero blocking OS system calls**.

## Voice Signal Architecture

The audio signal traverses the following stages sequentially:

```
[VCO 1 (polyBLEP)] ──▶ [ADSR 1] ──▶ [Mix Gain 1] ──┐
                                                    ├──▶ [Sum] ──▶ [VCF Filter] ──▶ [Volume Gain]
[VCO 2 (polyBLEP)] ──▶ [ADSR 2] ──▶ [Mix Gain 2] ──┘                                      │
                                                                                          ▼
[Stereo/Mono Out] ◀── [Stereo Reverb] ◀── [Ping-Pong Delay] ◀── [Redux Bitcrusher] ◀──────┘
```

---

## 1. Antialiased Generators (VCO 1 & VCO 2 with polyBLEP)

Both oscillators evaluate waveforms via normalized phase accumulators running in `[0.0, 1.0[` paired with high-performance **polyBLEP (Polynomial Bandlimited Step)** antialiasing:

Four selectable waveshapes:
- **Sine**: `sin(2 * pi * phase)` (pure spectral sine).
- **Saw**: `(2 * phase - 1) - polyBlep(phase, increment)` (rich harmonics free of digital aliasing).
- **Square**: binary pulse wave corrected by dual polyBLEP steps on rising and falling transitions (`phase` and `phase + 0.5`).
- **Triangle**: `1 - 4 * abs(phase - 0.5)` (warm, soft acoustic character).

### Pitch and Arpeggiator Tracking
Fundamental frequency `f` is computed from base pitch (C3 = 130.81 Hz), octave switch (−2 to +2), and note interval:

```cpp
f = 130.81 * std::exp2((double) octave + (double) note / 12.0);
```

When the **Arpeggiator** is engaged on a sequence, `advanceArp()` steps through the selected algorithmic pattern and musical scale, adding note offsets synchronously across both VCOs without altering their relative detune.

## 2. ADSR Envelopes

Each oscillator features an independent `juce::ADSR` envelope generator.
- Attack, decay, and release curves follow natural exponential decay characteristics.
- Temporal parameters use a logarithmic potentiometer taper (**skew factor 0.3**) providing fine resolution below 100 ms while allowing long swells up to 2 seconds.

---

## 3. VCF Filtering (State Variable TPT)

Filter stages utilize `juce::dsp::StateVariableTPTFilter<float>`.
Topology-preserving transform (TPT) ensures absolute numerical stability during high-speed cutoff modulation from LFOs:
- **LPF (Low-Pass Filter)**: 12 dB/octave attenuation of high frequencies.
- **HPF (High-Pass Filter)**: low-end sub cut and spectral thinning.
- Logarithmic sweep range from 20 Hz to 20,000 Hz.

---

## 4. Multi-FX: Redux (Bitcrusher & Decimator)

The Redux effect degrades audio through two distinct degradation stages:

1. **Downsampling (Temporal Decimation)**:
   A sample counter modulo `rate` latches and holds samples (Sample & Hold) over `N` frames (1 to 32).
2. **Bit-Depth Quantization**:
   ```cpp
   y = std::round (x * steps) / steps; // steps = 2^(bits - 1)
   ```
   Variable resolution from 2 bits (extreme digital crunch) to 16 bits (clean pass).
3. **Dry/Wet Crossfade**: phase-linear blend.

---

## 5. Multi-FX: True Ping-Pong Stereo Delay with Cross-Feedback

Delay line buffers are pre-allocated during `prepare()` with capacity for 2.5 seconds of audio:

- **True Cross-Feedback Ping-Pong**:
  Left channel echoes directly into the Right delay buffer, and Right echoes back into Left:
  ```cpp
  delayL[writePos] = in + std::tanh(outR * feedback);
  delayR[writePos] = std::tanh(outL * feedback);
  ```
- **Soft Hyperbolic Tangent (`tanh`) Saturation**:
  Non-linear saturation in the feedback loop prevents runaway feedback exceeding 0 dBFS even at maximum feedback settings (up to 90%).
- **Dual Stereo Mode**: switchable independent L/L and R/R parallel delay lines.
- **Instant Buffer Purge**: delay memory is instantly zeroed when toggled off (`delayBuffer.clear()`), avoiding stale audio bursts upon re-engagement.

---

## 6. Multi-FX: Stereo Reverb & Parameter Caching

Built upon `juce::dsp::Reverb`, spatial processing follows the delay line:
- **Room Mode**: short, dense early reflections suited for tight rhythm stabs.
- **Hall Mode**: expansive, cathedral-like decay tail.
- **Dirty Checking Optimization**: coefficients are recalculated only when reverb mode or mix changes, preventing comb-filter coefficient recomputation on every audio block.

---

## 7. Voice Sleep & Tail Management

To guarantee minimal CPU footprint (< 5% under load and 0.0% idle) without abruptly cutting off natural reverb or delay tails:

1. **Immediate Idle Bypass**:
   When envelopes are quiet (`!adsr1.isActive() && !adsr2.isActive()`) and delay/reverb are inactive, `Voice::renderChunk()` exits in **0 nanoseconds**.
2. **Decay Tail Management**:
   - Following note release, upstream synthesis is bypassed: **polyBLEP oscillators, ADSR envelopes, VCF filters, and Redux are completely skipped on silence**.
   - Delay and reverb decay tails continue ringing out naturally for up to 4.0 seconds.
3. **Acoustic Silence Detection**:
   When residual energy drops below -80 dBFS ($< 10^{-4}$), the voice is automatically put to sleep (`isVoiceActive = false`).
4. **Instant Transient Wakeup**:
   Any `noteOn()` event immediately wakes the voice with zero latency.

## 8. Summing, Mono Downmix & Soft Clipping Protection

At the conclusion of `processBlock()`:
1. **Automatic Mono Downmixing**:
   When instantiated on a mono DAW track, left and right channels from stereo delay and reverb are summed at 50% (`0.5f`), preserving all spatial effects without dropping the right channel.
2. **Headroom Attenuation & Soft Saturation**:
   A 6 dB headroom trim combined with smooth hyperbolic tangent (`std::tanh`) saturation prevents digital hard clipping when all 6 polyphonic voices sum simultaneously:
   ```cpp
   for (int ch = 0; ch < numChannels; ++ch)
   {
       auto* data = buffer.getWritePointer (ch);
       for (int i = 0; i < numSamples; ++i)
           data[i] = std::tanh (data[i]);
   }
   ```
3. **Studio Peak & RMS Monitoring at 60 Hz**:
   Real-time per-channel peak and RMS measurements are stored into thread-safe atomic floats (`peakDbL/R`, `rmsDbL/R`) with peak hold to drive the UI's calibrated dBFS meter and phase-locked oscilloscope.

## 9. Host Transport & BPM Sync

DAW timeline synchronization leverages JUCE's `juce::AudioPlayHead` interface queried on every `processBlock()` call:

```cpp
if (hostSyncParam != nullptr && hostSyncParam->load() > 0.5f)
{
    if (auto* playHead = getPlayHead())
    {
        if (auto posOpt = playHead->getPosition())
        {
            const double hostBpm = posOpt->getBpm().orFallback (120.0);
            const double ppqPos  = posOpt->getPpqPosition().orFallback (0.0);
            const bool isPlaying = posOpt->getIsPlaying();

            sequencer.syncToHost (ppqPos, hostBpm, isPlaying);
        }
    }
}
```

### Phase Locking Mechanism (PPQ)
- `ppqPosition` represents time in quarter notes (1.0 = 1 quarter note).
- Since Metulifer steps are sixteenth notes (1/4 of a quarter note), the absolute total step index is:
  `stepTotal = std::floor(ppqPos * 4.0)`
- Intra-step phase fraction aligns `sampleCounter` to the precise sample offset:
  `stepPhase = (ppqPos * 4.0) - std::floor(ppqPos * 4.0)`
  `sampleCounter = std::floor(stepPhase * stepLengthInSamples)`
- Each sequence `i` sets its current step modulo its active loop length:
  `currentStep[i] = stepTotal % length[i]`
This guarantees rock-solid rhythmic alignment even after jumping to arbitrary positions or when working with odd time signatures in the host DAW.

## 10. Real-Time Pitch Transposition (MIDI In)

Metulifer processes incoming MIDI note events arriving on the plugin's MIDI input:
- Incoming MIDI notes apply a global pitch transposition relative to middle **C3 (MIDI note 60)**:
  `deltaSemitones = midiNoteNumber - 60`
- This transposition offset is stored in `Voice::Params::transpose`.
- Both oscillators update their fundamental frequency instantaneously without memory allocation:
  `f = 130.81 * std::exp2((double) octave + (double) (note + arpOffset + transpose) / 12.0)`
- Enables real-time key changes and melodic transposition of running polyrhythms via external MIDI controllers or DAW piano-roll clips.

---

## 11. Multi-Channel Polyphonic Output (MIDI Out)

During every audio block, `SequencerEngine` collects active step gates into sample-accurate events `StepEvent`:
- Each active step emits a sample-accurate `noteOn` message on its designated MIDI channel:
  `midiChannel = trackIndex + 1` (channels 1 to 6)
- The pitch emitted reflects the track's root note combined with octave, note knob, and arpeggiator offsets.
- When playback is stopped or a track gate releases, corresponding `noteOff` and `allNotesOff` messages are dispatched to prevent stuck notes.
